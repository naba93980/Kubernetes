minikube start -> starts minikube
minikube status

kubectl get nodes -> details of nodes running
kubectl get deployments -o wide -> List all pods in ps output format with more information
kubectl version --output=json

CRUD:-
kubectl create/edit/delete deployment [name]

Status of different k8s components:-
kubectl get nodes|pods|services|deployments|replicatsets

Debugging pods:- 
kubectl logs [pod name]
kubectl exec -it [pod name] --bin/bash
